Sessions

It starts with a conversation. You tell me what's going on — a struggle you're in, something you're trying to practice, a life you're reaching for, whatever's actually alive for you. I listen, I ask questions, and we follow it. It usually goes somewhere neither of us expected. And at some point we hit a place worth marking — and a simple chant is born: a few words, a melody, something you can sing. I record it for you. If you want, it can join the gatherings where we sing together, and — once it's recorded in a studio — the chantbook.
