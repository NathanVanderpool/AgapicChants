Who this isn't for

This is not a replacement for professional therapy or an experience that will instantly heal you. It's best if you already have a basic spiritual and emotional literacy. If you don't, I recommend that we go into an accompaniment coaching relationship for a while and work up to making a chant together. Either way, let's talk.

About Nathan

I've been writing songs since I was 13, and I've spent the years since split between music and a quieter track: a doctorate in the sociology of religion, time in monasteries, and training in how people work through what's hard. I make songs with people, and then we sing them.
